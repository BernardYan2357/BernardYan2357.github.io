anygrasp
- 基于深度学习的抓取姿态估计方法，适用于复杂物体和多样化的抓取场景，由graspNet数据集训练得到
- [anygrasp](https://graspnet.net/anygrasp)
- [anygrasp github](https://github.com/graspnet/anygrasp_sdk)
- [anygrasp配置](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/1924881466229233373)
